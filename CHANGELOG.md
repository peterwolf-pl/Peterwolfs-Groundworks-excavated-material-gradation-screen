## [0.1.0+26.3] - 2026-10-08

### Added
- Initial release of **Peterwolf's Groundworks Gradation Screen** for Minecraft 26.3 Fabric.
- Mobile vibrating multi-deck screening plant for granular material classification.
- Vibrating wire mesh screen decks separating mixed excavated soils into gravel, sand, and fine dirt.
- Animated continuous conveyor belts and granular particle discharge streams.
- Full compatibility with Minecraft 26.3 overlay HUD messages.
